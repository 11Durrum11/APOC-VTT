Campaign map

The Realms as Forgotten Realms canon left them when the APOC hit — Shaun's thousand years of lore layer over this.

The Bastion of Last Hope — on this map, it is the city on the Sword Coast printed as Waterdeep: Waterdeep is its original name, and the Bastion of Last Hope is the name it carries now. Everything the map shows for Waterdeep — its streets, Undermountain below it, its history — is the Bastion's own pre-APOC past, altered by what the APOC did to it.

The world was decimated. Everywhere else is pretty much a ghost town or dead. Life did come back to the surface but diminished. Changed. Altered. The grey over this map is the Fog of Life — always grey, no sun, no stars — and the surviving cities are the pockets that held. What is dead, what is ruined, and what came back changed, the table marks as it learns.
